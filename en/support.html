Questions about the App and bug reports are accepted at the following contact.

Email transcribe_support@icloud.com

When reporting a bug

The following information helps identify the cause more quickly.

1. Device model and iOS version

2. App version (Settings → About → Version)

3. What you did and what happened

From “Settings → Bug reports & requests” in the app, you can send a report with operation logs attached (these do not include audio or transcription content).

Do not attach the recordings or transcription content themselves to email. What is needed for investigation is the sequence of operations and the operation logs, not the content of conversations.
