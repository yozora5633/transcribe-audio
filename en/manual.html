Transcribe Audio User Guide

Updated October 6, 2026 (for version 2.0)

The app starts in Simple Mode. Tap Choose a file or Record, pick the number of speakers, and you are ready. Transcription and speaker identification run on your device. No sign-in or API key is needed.

1. Start a transcription

  1. On the home screen, tap Choose a file or Record. To use the sound of a video, choose Import a video from Photos.
  2. Under Speakers on the home screen, choose 1, 2, or 3 or more.
  3. When transcription finishes, the text appears on the results screen. Use Copy or Share at the bottom. If you see Needs review, tap it to check those parts.

Record

  1. Tap Record on the home screen.
  2. Tap Start recording. When you are done, tap Stop recording.
  3. After you stop, transcription begins.

Recording continues while the screen is locked. Calls or system limits can interrupt it. There is no limit on recording length.

Use an audio file

  1. Tap Choose a file on the home screen and pick one audio file in the Files app.
  2. After you confirm the file name, transcription begins.

Download files stored in iCloud Drive or other cloud services to your device first. Any audio format your iPhone can play is supported. If a file cannot be imported, check that it plays in its original app, export it in another format, and try again.

Keep the app in the foreground until transcription finishes. The time needed depends on the length of the audio and your device. If transcription stops partway, start it again (it starts from the beginning, and your original audio is kept).

2. Detailed Mode

Tap Detailed Mode at the top right of the home screen, or Switch to detailed mode at the end of the Simple Mode settings. To go back, tap Simple Mode at the top right, or Return to Simple Mode in Settings.

  • Use How it's processed on the input card to choose the method, quality, and number of speakers in one place (see Methods and quality).
  • On supported devices (iOS 26 or later), text can appear while you record. Text shown during recording is provisional, so check the result after recording.
  • On the results screen, switch between Text and Segments. In Segments, you can replay the original audio with the time and speaker of each part.
  • Other › Advanced offers Export (Markdown and Text), Writing Tools, details of re-listening, and a new transcription.

3. Review and edit

Speech recognition can make mistakes. Check important numbers, names, and negations against the original audio.

  • Needs review: marks parts that may contain errors (they may also be correct). Tap it to open the list and play each part. Tap Keep as Is if it is correct, Use Suggestion if the suggested text is correct, or Dismiss Suggestion to remove a suggestion you do not need.
  • Edit the text: choose Other › Edit Text on the results screen. In Detailed Mode, you can also use the pencil button on each segment.
  • Search: search the text to find the parts you want to check.

4. Copy, share, and export

  • Use Copy at the bottom of the results screen to copy the text, and Share to send it to another app. To use only part of the text, touch and hold to select it.
  • Choose Share › Save Text to a File to save it in the Files app. Other › Details › Export Detailed Record saves a record with times and processing settings. In Detailed Mode, Other › Advanced › Export lets you choose Markdown or text.
  • In Detailed Mode, Other › Advanced › Refine with Writing Tools opens a separate draft that you can polish with the system Writing Tools. Your original transcript does not change. Available features depend on your device and system settings. If you choose ChatGPT or a similar option in Writing Tools, text may be sent off your device according to your system settings (see section 4 of the Privacy Policy).

When you choose to share or save to a cloud service, the text is sent to the app or service you chose.

5. Identify speakers

  • 1 person (no separation): no speaker names are added.
  • 2 people: suited to conversations and phone calls.
  • 3 or more: the number of speakers is estimated automatically.

Speaker identification runs on your device. By default, two methods check each other, and parts where they disagree are marked Needs review. In Detailed Mode, Speaker identification in Advanced Settings lets you use only one of them. Similar voices or people talking at the same time can be mixed up, so listen again to check. To change speaker names, choose Other › Rename Speakers. To try again with a different number of speakers, choose Other › Transcribe again.

Speakers can be identified in audio up to 60 minutes long. For longer audio, transcribe with 1 person, or split the audio into parts of 60 minutes or less before importing it.

6. Methods and quality (Detailed Mode)

  • Whisper, Highest: the most accurate option and the usual recommendation. The model is included in the app.
  • Whisper, High Accuracy: a little faster than Highest. The first time you use it, the model (about 630 MB) is downloaded from Hugging Face. You can also download it in advance from Settings.
  • iOS built-in: uses Apple's speech recognition on your device. It is recommended for supported iPhone 12 and earlier models. The first time you use it, resources needed for recognition may be downloaded from Apple.

Whisper can transcribe audio up to 4 hours long. For longer audio, split it into parts of 4 hours or less, or choose iOS built-in.

Re-listening, which tries again on parts that could not be recognized, can be turned on in Advanced Settings (it is off by default). Re-listened results can also contain errors, so check them against the original audio. To restore the original settings, use Reset Transcription Settings in the Detailed Mode settings. Recordings, history, dictionaries, the retention period, and the app lock are kept.

7. Register names and terms (Dictionary)

In Simple Mode, open Settings › Data › Register Names and Terms (Dictionary). In Detailed Mode, open Advanced Settings › Dictionary. Enter the correct spelling and, if needed, the reading and spellings that are often misrecognized. You can create several dictionaries and import many words at once from a CSV file (in the form “spelling,reading”). Dictionaries are stored on your device. Registering a word does not guarantee that it will always be recognized correctly.

8. Recordings, history, and retention

  • History: finished transcriptions are kept in History. Open it from History on the home screen or Other › History on the results screen, and search by file name or text. Opening an item lets you continue listening, editing, and reviewing.
  • Where recordings are saved: recordings are saved in the Files app under Transcribe Audio › Recordings. If you choose another folder in Settings, a copy is also saved there.
  • Retention period: choose it in Settings › Recording and Data Storage. New installations use 90 days. Recordings, results, and exported files older than the period are cleaned up automatically. Before shortening the period, read the confirmation of what will be deleted. Files copied to another folder are not cleaned up automatically.
  • App lock: on the same screen, you can lock the app with Face ID or similar.
  • What stays: closing the results screen or going home does not delete recordings or history. Deleting history does not delete recordings in the Files app or copies in other folders.

9. FAQ

Can I use it entirely on my device?

Yes. Transcription and speaker identification run on your device, and audio and text are not sent to outside services for them. The app uses the network only to download models or recognition resources, to fetch files stored in the cloud, and for actions you choose, such as sharing or Writing Tools. See the Privacy Policy for details.

Does it cost anything?

Transcription in this version has no additional charge. Data charges for downloads and sharing depend on your plan.

Can text appear while I record?

Yes, in Detailed Mode on supported devices (iOS 26 or later). In Simple Mode, the text appears after recording.

Can I turn off the screen or switch to another app?

Recording continues while the screen is locked, but calls or system limits can interrupt it. Keep the app in the foreground until transcription finishes.

Can I resume a transcription that stopped partway?

Not in this version. Starting again processes the audio from the beginning. Your original audio is kept.

It takes a long time or seems stuck

Long audio and the Highest quality take longer. Check the stage, elapsed time, and progress on screen, and wait. Processing may also take longer than usual when your device is hot.

Which audio files can I use? Is there a length limit?

Choose one file at a time from the Files app. Any audio format your iPhone can play is supported. Whisper can transcribe audio up to 4 hours long, and speakers can be identified in audio up to 60 minutes long.

I cannot start recording

Check that the app is allowed to use the microphone. If you did not allow it, change it from Open Microphone Settings on the recording screen. If a message about storage space or the save location appears, follow it.

English words or sentences that make no sense appear in the Japanese text

They may be recognition errors. They can also be real English words or names, so the app does not delete them automatically. Stock phrases (such as “thank you for watching”) can also appear where there is no speech. Listen to the original audio, for example from Needs review, and edit the text if needed.

Can I use the app in English?

Display language in Settings opens the iOS language setting for this app. The display language and the language of speech recognition are separate. Speech processing is designed for Japanese.

Can I make the text larger?

In Simple Mode, use Settings (gear) › Text size. In Detailed Mode, use Advanced Settings › Display and storage.

How do I report a problem?

Use Bug reports & requests in Settings to send an email. You choose which records to attach before sending. See also the Support page.
