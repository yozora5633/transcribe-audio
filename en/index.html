Transcribe Audio

Record, transcribe, and tell speakers apart

Transcribe Audio transcribes your recordings and audio files on your iPhone or iPad. Telling speakers apart, reviewing and editing, and sharing all happen in one app. It suits meetings, interviews, lectures, and reporting.

The screens above show the Japanese display. The app also displays in English.

What you can do

Transcribe on your device

Record on the spot, or import an audio file or a video (from the Files app or Photos), and the app transcribes it on your device. The speech recognition model is included in the app, and no sign-in is needed.

Tell speakers apart

The transcript is split by speaker, such as “Speaker 1” and “Speaker 2” (choose 1, 2, or 3 or more people). Passages the app is unsure about are marked “Needs review”, so you can see right away what to check. You can rename speakers later.

Review, edit, and share

Edit the text while replaying the original audio segment by segment. Search within the transcript, copy or share it, or export it as text or Markdown. Register names and terms in the dictionary to keep spellings consistent.

Long recordings

Recording continues while the screen is locked, and there is no limit on audio length. On iOS 26 or later, text can appear while you record (live transcription).

Simple and detailed modes

Start in simple mode: two buttons, “Choose a file” and “Record”, and the only choice is the number of speakers. Detailed mode lets you choose the transcription method and quality.

History and app lock

Transcriptions stay in History and can be found by file name or text. Choose a retention period to clean up old recordings and results automatically. Lock the app with Face ID or similar.

Privacy

  • Transcription runs on your device. The app has no feature that sends audio or text to external servers.
  • Recordings, transcriptions, history, and the dictionary are stored on your device. There is no developer server, and the developer never receives or sees the contents.
  • No ads and no tracking. See the Privacy Policy for details.

Requirements

  • iPhone and iPad (iOS 17.4 or later).
  • The display is available in Japanese and English. Speech processing is designed for Japanese.
  • How long transcription takes depends on the length of the audio and the device’s performance.
